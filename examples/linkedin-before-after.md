> Leveraging cutting-edge AI, this tool helps students master knowledge questions and connect Areas of Knowledge like never before. The future of education is here!

>
> #AI #EdTech #Innovation #IB #FutureOfLearning

## After (with skill)

> My TOK teacher had a complaint: students can define every Area of Knowledge and connect none of them to real life.
>
> So I built a GPT that does one thing — it takes a knowledge question and forces you to argue it through real-world examples before it lets you near a conclusion. No essay writing. Just the thinking part.
>
> Teachers at my school now use it in class. The students who benefit most aren't the strugglers — it's the strong writers who were coasting on structure instead of ideas.
>
> Narrow tools that refuse to do the work for you beat general chatbots for learning. That's the whole finding.

## What changed

- The hook is the teacher's problem, not the announcement of the builder's feelings.
- "Revolutionizing education" became "teachers at my school use it in class" — smaller claim, infinitely more credible.
- The reader who never touches the tool still leaves with an idea (narrow > general for learning).
- Zero emoji armor, zero hashtag spray, zero "this is just the beginning".
