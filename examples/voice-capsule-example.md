# Example — voice-capsule (excerpt)

**Input:** 6 LinkedIn posts by a student builder (the two in `skills/linkedin-signal-posts/examples.md` plus four like them).

## Output capsule (excerpt)

**Rules (each held in 3+ samples):**
1. Opens with a problem or a concrete constraint — never an announcement. ("My TOK teacher had a complaint:", "Three weekends, one rule:")
2. One technical or process detail per post that a non-user still learns from.
3. Claims sized to evidence: "teachers at my school use it" — never a round number that begs scrutiny.
4. Ends on a transferable insight or one flat sentence. Never a question begging comments.

**Never-do list:** "thrilled to announce" · emoji armor · hashtag spray · fake humility ("so humbled") · "just my two cents" hedging.

**Annotated excerpt:**
> "the architecture is the privacy policy"
*Signature move: compressing a design decision into a slogan-sized claim. Appears in 4 of 6 samples in different forms. Reuse the move, not the phrase.*

**Validation:** test paragraph drafted on a new topic; first version opened with "I built…" — patched rule 1 to explicitly cover project-launch posts, regenerated, matched.

## Why this works
Every rule is checkable against the samples, the voice lives in the annotated excerpts rather than adjectives, and validation caught a drift the rules missed — which is the point of validating.
